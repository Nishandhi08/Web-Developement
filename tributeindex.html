Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Life and Achievements

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He was an aerospace scientist and played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs.

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007 and inspired millions of students through his speeches, books, and vision for a developed India.

Some of his famous books include Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020. His dedication to education and innovation continues to inspire people worldwide.
